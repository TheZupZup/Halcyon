Linthra 0.2.5

- Adds a persistent desktop navigation shell on Linux, so the sidebar stays put on wide windows.
- Adapts album, artist, and Now Playing layouts to large screens, with denser grids and side-by-side detail views.
- Puts playback controls on the now-playing bar and promotes Folders to a top-level destination.
- Fixes the Linux window identity and app icon so launchers and task switchers show Linthra correctly.
- Shows device-wide music access on Android as an explicit, separate choice from picking a folder.
- Fixes Android Auto stalling on the Songs list with large libraries.
- Reports Jellyfin as unavailable when the server cannot be reached, instead of always connected.
